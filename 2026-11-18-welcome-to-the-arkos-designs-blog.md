This is a sample post. Replace it with your own, or delete this file and add a new one.

Posts are plain text files in the `_posts` folder. Each file name starts with the date, then a short title, and ends in `.md`, like `2026-12-02-choosing-pa6-cf.md`. At the top of each file, add a title between two lines of three dashes.

## What to write about

- How a part was designed and why it was printed in a particular material
- Notes on filaments, tolerances and what worked
- Behind-the-scenes looks at plasma cutting and bending jobs

Photos go in `assets/img`, and you can show one in a post like this: `![Description](/assets/img/your-photo.jpg)`.
